Be — a gentle guide
============================================================

One small reminder from biology each morning, and the act it suggests. This sheet is everything you need; no answer here is ever wrong.

In this guide

  - What Be is

  - Opening it

  - The three rooms

  - When a difficult feeling arrives

  - Your choices, always yours

  - Small comforts & where to ask for aid

1 · What Be is
------------------------------------------------------------

Be is named twice — for the bee, small keeper of whole ecosystems, and for being: a body, an organism, part of the whole. Each morning it offers one piece of research about how bodies actually work — sunlight, breath, rest, each other — translated into a five-minute act. Take it or leave it; both are welcome.

2 · Opening it
------------------------------------------------------------

  - From a link you were given: tap it. To keep Be on your phone — iPhone: Share ⬆ → Add to Home Screen. Android: menu ⋮ → Install app. After opening once, it works even without a signal.

  - From a file you were given: double-click Be App (offline).html. It opens in your browser and works entirely on your computer.

3 · The three rooms
------------------------------------------------------------

Today — the morning's finding, its source in parentheses, and the small act it suggests. Answer "I'll try it" or "Not today"; you can change your mind anytime.

Hive — who's alongside you today. No streaks, no scores; a shared act is an invitation, never an expectation.

You — your choices: the morning note, quiet evenings, what your circle sees, larger text. Everything starts off until you say so. Below the switches, "What Be is learning — beside you" shows plain counts of what you tried or rested from; "Take my counts with me" saves them as a small text file, and "Ask Be to forget everything" clears it all after one plain question.

4 · When a difficult feeling arrives
------------------------------------------------------------

Anger, grief, static — Be treats these as biology arriving, not flaws. On Today, "When a charge arrives" walks with you, gently: name the feeling, sense how strong it is, then let it move — a few long breaths, a minute by a window, a short walk. "Still here" is as honored an ending as "It moved".

5 · Your choices, always yours
------------------------------------------------------------

  - Nothing is on until you turn it on. "No" and "not yet" are always safe answers.

  - What you do in Be stays on your device unless you switch on "Let my circle see how I'm doing" — and even then, only whether you're trying or resting today leaves, never what or why.

  - Be follows your device's own comfort settings — larger text, reduced motion, higher contrast, screen readers.

6 · Small comforts & where to ask for aid
------------------------------------------------------------

  - Text feels small — You → "Larger text", or your device's own zoom.

  - It forgot my choices — private/incognito windows don't remember; open Be in a normal window.

  - The file won't open — right-click → Open With → any browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge).

  - An old version keeps showing — close it fully and reopen while online; it quietly refreshes itself.

  - Anything else — ask the person who shared Be with you; they have the fuller ledger. Asking for aid is very much in the spirit of the thing.

  TIP: Be offers reminders from research, plainly told. It isn't medical advice, and it never rushes you.
