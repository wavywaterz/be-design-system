Default is no

When consent isn't clear, don't. Unchecked boxes, honored quiet hours, nothing renews silently.

Ask for the minimum

The least data, attention, and permission the moment needs — never a blank check.

Propose, don't act

Be suggests; the person decides. Nothing irreversible without a plain, in-the-moment ask.

Honesty over polish

State limits beside strengths. "The evidence is early" is a good sentence; "guaranteed" never is.
