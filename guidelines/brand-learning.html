Be absorbs experience the way a hive does — many small honest reports, no names attached. The person is never the product; the pattern is the teacher.

Learns beside you

All learning starts on the device — what was tried, what was rested — visible to the person in plain counts.

Counts, never content

What can be shared is arithmetic ("tried 3, rested 1") — never words, notes, names, or times.

Opt-in, blended, many

Sharing is off until switched on; shared counts join only in aggregates large enough that no one stands out.

Easy to vanish

"Ask Be to forget everything" is one plain ask, honored instantly, no guilt — and "take my counts with me" hands over the arithmetic first, if wanted.
