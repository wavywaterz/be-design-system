A negative charge — anger, grief, static — is biology arriving, not a flaw. Met with awareness it becomes signal, not harm. Settled bodies settle rooms; nothing is wasted, not even this.

Acknowledge

Welcome it in without shame. "Not now" is honored too.

Comprehend

Name it, gently — words quiet the alarm. (Psychological Science, 2007)

Measure

How much is it carrying, 1–5? Any answer is right.

Manage

Breath, distance, or movement — sized to the charge. (Cell Reports Medicine, 2023)
