Seen

AA contrast on every surface; visible honey focus ring on all controls; never color alone — states pair with borders, checkmarks, and words. Larger-text switch in-app; OS zoom and high-contrast honored.

Heard & read aloud

Real roles and names throughout — tabs, switches, dialogs, live status toasts. Every image has alt text; every control a label a screen reader can speak.

Touched

Hit targets 44px and up on mobile. Full keyboard paths: Tab reaches everything, arrows move between tabs, nothing requires a hover or a gesture.

Settled

Reduced-motion preference collapses all animation to instant. Quiet hours honored. Nothing flashes, autoplays, or counts down.

Understood

Same rooms in the same order, always; plain literal words; 2–3 choices per moment; no timers, nothing expires. Lineage: W3C COGA patterns for cognitive & learning differences.

Felt

The words themselves carry warmth — held, embraced, welcome — so meaning arrives even when a channel doesn't. Touch language is always asked-for; a giggle is honored as biology.
